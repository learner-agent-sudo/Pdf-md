Text-based PDFs and .docx files convert cleanly, including non-English text. Enable OCR (and pick the language) to read scanned/image PDFs and image files — printed text in the selected language works well; handwriting and signatures do not. OCR is noticeably slower. The old binary .doc format isn't supported (save it as .docx first).
