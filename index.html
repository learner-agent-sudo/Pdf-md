Working…

Text-based PDFs and .docx files convert cleanly. Enable OCR to read scanned/image PDFs and image files (printed text works well; handwriting and signatures do not). OCR is English-only and noticeably slower. The old binary .doc format isn't supported (save it as .docx first).
